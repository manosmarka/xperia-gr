# Xperia.gr Design System

**Vibe:** Ultra-clean Apple-style glassmorphism with Vibrant 3D elements.

## 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation
When generating screens, ALWAYS strictly follow this block:

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Platform:** Web, Desktop-first responsive.
- **Palette (Vibrant Glass):** 
  - Background: Space Gray (#1C1C1E) or Frost White (#F5F5F7) depending on context. Let's force Dark Mode: Deep Midnight (#000000).
  - Primary Brand / 3D Highlights: Vivid Purple (#BF5AF2) merging into Electric Cyan (#64D2FF).
  - Surface: Frosted Glass (White/10% with heavy backdrop blur), Apple-style cleanly segmented cards.
- **Styles:** Very generous padding, Roundness ROUND_EIGHT or fully rounded buttons, soft diffuse shadows mimicking glowing 3D objects, very high contrast text. 
- **Typography:** System/Inter style, exceptionally clean, lots of negative space.
- **Structure Notes:** Prominently feature sleek 3D graphic placeholders.

**Global Navigation & Footer Rules:**
Include a simple top glass sticky nav: "Xperia.gr logo, Kids Seminars, Adult Seminars, Business AI, Learn, News".
